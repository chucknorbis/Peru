After the long arc from Madrid you land at the gleaming new Jorge Chavez terminal. A pre-booked van (one minivan fits all 7) rolls you south to Miraflores, Lima's cliff-top district, safest and most walkable in the capital.

Today is deliberately gentle: check in, then walk the Malecon - ten kilometres of manicured clifftop park where paragliders hang over the grey-green Pacific. Finish with the trip's first ceviche; in the world's gastronomic capital even a mid-range lunch counter will astonish you.

Field notes

  • Sol (PEN) ~4.0 = 1 EUR; best rates at Miraflores casas de cambio, not the airport
  • Uber/Cabify are the safe taxi option citywide
  • Never drink tap water anywhere in Peru
  • Pre-book the airport van - arrivals-hall touts overcharge
  • Claro/Movistar prepaid data ~10 EUR covers the whole trip

Photo tips

  • Golden hour (17:00-18:00) on the Malecon: paragliders backlit over the ocean
  • Parque del Amor mosaic wall - wide angle, sunset behind the El Beso statue
  • Larcomar terraces: frame the cliffs curving north with a long exposure

Gear for today

  • Light jacket - the sea mist is cool
  • Comfortable walking shoes from day one
  • Power bank (long travel day)

Eat here

  • Cevicheria for lunch-style dinner - order ceviche clasico + chicha morada
  • Anticuchos (grilled beef-heart skewers) from an evening street cart
  • Pisco sour aperitif - it was invented in this city
