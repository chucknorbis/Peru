The best museum for last: the Larco, a flower-draped mansion over pre-Columbian storerooms, holding five thousand years of Peruvian art - Moche portrait ceramics of impossible realism, gold funerary regalia, and open storage with thirty thousand pieces on view. Its garden cafe is one of Lima's loveliest lunches.

Then the dinner the group has earned: ceviche, tiradito, arroz con mariscos and a round of pisco sours - a toast to twenty days, four climate zones, and roughly a thousand photographs each.
