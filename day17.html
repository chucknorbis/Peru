Early along the canyon rim - a gorge twice as deep as the Grand Canyon, terraced by pre-Inca farmers and still worked today - to be standing at Cruz del Condor by eight, when the sun warms the walls and the thermals begin.

Then they come: Andean condors, wings over three metres, launching from cliff nests to circle at eye level, sometimes near enough to hear the wind tearing through their feathers. It is one of nature's most reliable great spectacles, and it never feels routine.
