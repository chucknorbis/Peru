A pre-dawn start for the highest moment of the trip. The mineral bands of Vinicunca - iron red, sulphur yellow, copper turquoise - were hidden under glacier ice until barely a decade ago.

The walk is short but the air holds half the oxygen of home: go slowly, breathe rhythmically, and the reward at 5,036 metres is a striped mountain that looks painted, with the glaciated giant Ausangate filling the horizon. The Red Valley just over the ridge is, arguably, even better.

Field notes

  • Group tour ~30 EUR incl. transport + meals + entry; private 7-seat van ~170 EUR total
  • Horses available on-site (~15 EUR) for most of the ascent
  • This is the skip-day if anyone struggled with altitude - it is optional beauty
  • After rain the colours mute - tours can shift a day if forecast is bad

Photo tips

  • Shoot the stripes AFTER 9:30 when sun clears the ridge - before that they are flat
  • Red Valley: saturated ochre bowls, best wide + polarising effect
  • Alpaca portraits on the walk-in, long lens / zoom

Gear for today

  • Gloves, beanie, 4 layers for dawn
  • SPF50 - you will burn at 5,000 m even at -5 C
  • 2 L water + coca sweets
  • Sunglasses cat.3+

Eat here

  • Tour breakfast + lunch included - eat light before the climb
  • Big comfort dinner back in Cusco: soup + lomo saltado
