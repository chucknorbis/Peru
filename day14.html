Six hours southwest across the altiplano, skirting salt flats and flamingo lagoons, then down between the volcanoes into Arequipa - Peru's second city and its most elegant, carved from white volcanic sillar under the perfect cone of El Misti.

At a mere 2,335 metres the air feels luxuriously thick. The evening belongs to the Plaza de Armas, arguably the most beautiful square in Peru, its two-storey colonnades glowing as the volcanoes go pink behind.
