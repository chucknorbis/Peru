The two-day Colca excursion climbs over the Andes' shoulder: through the national vicuna reserve, where the wild, dainty ancestors of the alpaca graze against a backdrop of cones, to Patapampa at 4,910 metres - a dizzy roll-call of eight snowcapped volcanoes.

Descend to Chivay in the green upper canyon, and spend the afternoon exactly as you should: soaking in the 38-degree La Calera pools beside the river as the canyon rim goes gold and the first hard stars come out.

Field notes

  • Patapampa is a brief stop - fine even if tired; it is the 2nd-highest point of the trip
  • Colca entry ticket ~17 EUR collected on the road - keep it for tomorrow
  • Swimwear + towel for the springs (entry ~4 EUR, towel rental available)
  • Chivay nights hit 0 C - the pools at dusk are the warm place

Photo tips

  • Vicunas: long zoom, keep the volcano cone in frame
  • Patapampa: the stacked stone apachetas + summits panorama
  • Steam over the pools at dusk - moody phone shots work great

Gear for today

  • Swimwear + towel + flip-flops
  • Down jacket for the evening
  • Headlamp for the walk back from the pools

Eat here

  • Trout or alpaca in Chivay
  • Mate de coca before sleep - it is a cold-night town
