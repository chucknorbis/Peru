Peru: the Highlands,
the Lost City & the Green Sea

Machu Picchu at dawn, a 5,000-metre painted mountain, giant otters in an Amazon oxbow, reed islands on the world's highest lake, and condors at arm's length over a canyon twice the Grand Canyon's depth — one route, twenty days, the start of the Andean dry season.
