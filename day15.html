Santa Catalina is not a building but a walled citadel of twenty thousand square metres, where nuns lived in seclusion for four centuries: ochre and cobalt lanes, geranium courtyards, smoke-blackened kitchens - a photographer's fever dream. Allow three hours.

Then meet 'Juanita', the Inca girl sacrificed on Ampato's summit five hundred years ago and preserved by the ice almost perfectly. Lunch is the day's main event: rocoto relleno, chupe de camarones and adobo in a traditional picanteria - Arequipa feeds Peru its best regional table.
