Forty minutes in the air undo three thousand metres of altitude: from Andean chill into the thick, loud heat of Madre de Dios. A motorised canoe takes you up the river highway - capybaras on the banks, herons lifting ahead of the bow - to a stilted, screen-walled lodge in the Tambopata buffer forest.

At dusk the sky goes copper, macaws commute overhead in pairs, and after dark the guide's torch finds the ember-orange eyes of caimans along the banks. The jungle at night is the loudest silence you will ever hear.

Field notes

  • 3d/2n all-inclusive lodge: budget ~170 EUR pp, mid-range ~280 EUR pp - book ahead for 7
  • Pack a soft duffel; main luggage stores free in town
  • Yellow-fever vaccination recommended for this region
  • Lodge electricity often runs only a few evening hours - power banks
  • Malaria risk is low but discuss prophylaxis with a travel doctor

Photo tips

  • Bow-of-the-canoe shots: river bends + storm light
  • Sunset cruise: silhouettes of island trees, 17:00-17:30
  • Night torch beams - crank ISO, brace on the gunwale

Gear for today

  • 50%+ DEET repellent
  • Long light sleeves + trousers (bite + sun)
  • Head torch
  • Dry bag / zip-locks for phones
  • Rain poncho

Eat here

  • Lodge meals included - jungle-fish, plantain, exotic fruit
  • Try camu-camu and aguaje juices
