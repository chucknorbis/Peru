One last climb - the canopy tower at first light, the forest canopy rolling to every horizon like a green sea - then the boat glides downriver and the afternoon flight lifts you back to the Andes.

Cusco gives you a final evening: proper souvenir hunting (real baby-alpaca on Triunfo street, silver in San Blas) and a farewell feast in a picanteria, where platters arrive sized for exactly a table of seven.
