Booking order (urgency first)

  1. Machu Picchu entries x7 + Huayna Picchu - tuboleto.cultura.pe, the moment dates are fixed
  2. Long-haul CLJ-MAD + MAD-LIM r/t - when 2027 schedules open (~mid-2026 for these dates)
  3. Trains Ollantaytambo-Aguas Calientes r/t x7 (PeruRail / IncaRail)
  4. Domestic flights: LIM-CUZ (May 2), CUZ-PEM r/t (May 8/10), AQP-LIM (May 18)
  5. Tambopata lodge 3d/2n for 7
  6. Rooms: Lima x2 · Valley x2 · Aguas Calientes x1 · Cusco x4 · Puno x2 · Arequipa x4 (Chivay via tour) · Lima x2
  7. Bookable locally: Ruta del Sol bus (May 11), Titicaca boat (May 12), Colca 2-day (May 15-16), Vinicunca (May 7)

Health & altitude

  • Yellow-fever vaccine advised for the Amazon leg; see a travel doctor 6-8 weeks out
  • Discuss acetazolamide for altitude; the itinerary itself is the main protection (valley-first)
  • Travel insurance must cover trekking to 5,100 m - check the fine print
  • 3 litres of water per person per day above 3,000 m, no exceptions
  • National Alliance-grade rule: any severe headache + vomiting at altitude = descend, not endure

Money

  • Sol (PEN) ~4.0 = 1 EUR; cards fine in cities, cash for markets, villages, tips
  • Best exchange: Miraflores + Cusco casas de cambio; count notes at the counter
  • ATMs: BCP and Interbank charge least; withdraw large, few times
  • Shared kitty for vans, guides and group meals - one treasurer, settled twice

Documents & group logistics

  • Passports valid 6+ months; Romanians visa-free 90 days; keep any migration slip
  • Photocopies + phone scans of all 7 passports in two people's clouds
  • Always quote 'private van, 7 pax' - it beats 7 tour seats on price and comfort
  • Offline: Maps.me / Google offline areas for Cusco, Arequipa, Puno before leaving wifi
