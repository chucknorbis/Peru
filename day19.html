A civilised morning, then the flight tracks the desert coast back to Lima for the final two nights. The afternoon is unstructured: cliff-mall shoppers to Larcomar, walkers to the full Malecon they only sampled on day one.

The sight worth keeping: Huaca Pucllana, a vast adobe pyramid of the Lima culture rising surreally from the middle of the residential grid, floodlit after dark. Night circuits run Wednesday to Sunday only, and today is Tuesday - so the visit is slotted for tomorrow evening, before the farewell feast.
