Morning among the canary-yellow arcades of the colonial centre: the Plaza Mayor, the Cathedral, and San Francisco's eerie catacombs stacked with the bones of an estimated twenty-five thousand people.

May 1 is Labour Day - museums may shut, but plazas, churches and restaurants stay lively. In late afternoon shift to Barranco, the bohemian seaside quarter of street art and galleries, and stay through sunset: no district in Lima has more atmosphere per square metre.

Field notes

  • Catacombs: guided entry only (~4 EUR), English tours every 30 min
  • Front pockets for phones in the crowded centre
  • Changing of the guard at the Palace around midday
  • Reserve dinner for 7 - holiday evenings fill up

Photo tips

  • Catacomb bone-wells (no flash) - brace the phone on the railing
  • Yellow arcades of Plaza Mayor: symmetry shot from the fountain
  • Barranco murals in golden hour; the Bridge of Sighs from below on the Bajada

Gear for today

  • Cash in small notes for street snacks

Eat here
