A slow city day in the Inca capital, where perfect pre-Columbian walls carry colonial churches like riders. Qorikancha is the thesis of the whole city: the Sun Temple the conquistadors stripped of gold, with a convent built straight on top - and the Inca stones underneath still truer than anything above them.

Uphill, Sacsayhuaman's zig-zag ramparts stack hundred-tonne boulders fitted without mortar; walk back down through San Blas, the balconied artisan quarter, into the great plaza at blue hour.

Field notes

  • Sacsayhuaman is on your Boleto Turistico
  • The 12-angled stone is free - on Hatun Rumiyoc lane
  • Cusco nights near 0 C; hotels lack central heating - request heaters
  • Chocolate & Pisco museums near the plaza do free tastings

Photo tips

  • Qorikancha's curved wall - the finest masonry in Peru, side-lit morning
  • Sacsayhuaman zig-zags with Cusco bowl behind, 16:00 light
