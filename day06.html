The day the whole journey is built around. Ride one of the first buses up and enter at dawn, when mist tears off the peaks and the citadel appears in pieces - a wall, a terrace, then the whole impossible city.

Walk the upper circuit for the panorama, then descend past the Temple of the Sun, the Intihuatana stone and the Condor. If you booked Huayna Picchu, forty-five steep minutes buy you the view straight down onto the ruins. By tonight you sleep in Cusco, altitude-proof at last.

Field notes

  • Timed, circuit-specific tickets capped daily - book 7 together 2-3 months out at tuboleto.cultura.pe
  • Huayna Picchu must be chosen at purchase (+~10 EUR)
  • Passports required at the gate (souvenir stamp available)
  • No toilets inside; no tripods, drones or (officially) food
  • Licensed gate guide ~55 EUR split 7 ways - worth every sol
  • Mist until 08:00 is normal in May - do not panic, it lifts

Photo tips

  • Guard House at 06:30: the classic frame, mist still in the gorge
  • Huayna Picchu summit: shoot vertical to compress citadel + valley
  • Llamas on the terraces - low angle, city behind
  • Golden stone close-ups in late-morning side light

Gear for today

  • Layers: 5 C at dawn to 24 C by noon
  • Rain shell (cloud forest!)
  • Passports + both tickets
  • Water + hidden snacks
  • Bug spray - sandflies at the ruins

Eat here

  • Pre-dawn: bakery sandwiches + coca tea
  • Celebration late lunch in Aguas Calientes - trout or pizza, you earned either
