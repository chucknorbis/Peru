A gentle last morning - paragliders over the Costa Verde, brunch above the ocean - then the airport with a generous margin: allow ninety minutes for traffic and three hours for checking in a seven-person group with hold luggage.

The evening flight crosses the Atlantic overnight into Madrid on Friday afternoon, in time for the 20:50 Wizz hop that lands you in Cluj at half past one in the morning. Twenty days, one empire, zero regrets.
