A full valley circuit by private van. Pisac stacks sprawling terraces beneath a hilltop citadel, above one of Peru's most famous artisan markets - and today is Sunday, its biggest day.

Moray's concentric rings - each level its own microclimate - were most likely an Inca agricultural laboratory. Finish at the Salineras: four and a half thousand blinding-white salt pans terraced down a hillside, harvested by the same families since before the Incas.

Field notes

  • Buy the full Boleto Turistico (~35 EUR, 10 days) - covers Pisac, Moray, Ollantaytambo + Cusco sites
  • Maras salt pans: separate small fee ~3 EUR
  • Day van split by 7: ~11 EUR each
  • Altitude sun burns in 20 minutes - SPF50 and a hat
  • Market is cash-only; friendly haggling expected

Photo tips

  • Pisac terraces from the citadel rim, morning shadows
  • Moray: shoot from the top lip with a person on the lowest ring for scale
  • Maras at 15:30-16:30 - low sun sidelights the salt crusts

Gear for today

  • Hat + SPF50 + lip balm
  • Small soles for the market
  • Grippy shoes - salt paths are slick

Eat here
