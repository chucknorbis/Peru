Climb the two hundred and fifty steps of the fortress where Manco Inca actually defeated a Spanish army in 1537, up to the six rose-granite monoliths of the unfinished Sun Temple, dragged here from a quarry across the valley.

Then one of the world's great short railways: an hour and three quarters squeezed into the Urubamba gorge as highland scrub gives way to orchids and cloud forest, ending at the foot of Machu Picchu itself.

Field notes

  • Trains ~50-70 EUR each way (Expedition/Voyager class) - 7 seats together sell out months ahead
  • Train luggage limit 5-7 kg - big bags stay at the Valley hotel
  • Buy tomorrow's Consettur bus tickets tonight (~11 EUR one-way)
  • Pick a hotel slightly uphill from the plaza - quieter
  • Skip the town thermal baths - underwhelming

Photo tips

  • Fortress terraces from the granary side at 08:00 - long diagonal shadows
  • Sun Temple monoliths close-up: the knife-fit joints
  • Open train windows after Km 82 - river rapids + peaks

Gear for today

  • Overnight pack only: dawn layers, rain shell, passports
  • Train tickets + Machu Picchu tickets printed AND on phones

Eat here

  • Carb-heavy dinner: pasta or lomo saltado - fuel for dawn
