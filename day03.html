An eighty-minute flight replaces a 20-hour bus and lands you at 3,400 metres - you will feel it in the first staircase. The trick, and the reason tonight is not spent in Cusco: drive immediately down into the Sacred Valley, six hundred metres lower, where acclimatisation is far kinder.

Ollantaytambo is the best-preserved living Inca town in existence - water still races through 500-year-old stone channels beside every lane. Walk slowly, drink coca tea, and let your blood catch up with the altitude.

Field notes

  • 48-hour altitude rules: no alcohol, light food, huge amounts of water, zero exertion
  • Ask your doctor about acetazolamide before the trip; coca tea genuinely helps
  • Morning flights are cancelled less (afternoon winds at CUZ)
  • Private 7-seat van airport -> Ollantaytambo ~40 EUR total
  • Nights drop near freezing - hotels rarely have heating; ask for extra blankets

Photo tips

  • Left-side plane window: glaciated peaks 20 min before landing
  • Chinchero plateau: the Urubamba range across yellow fields
  • Blue doorways + Inca walls in Ollantaytambo's Qosqo Ayllu quarter

Gear for today

  • Warm layer + beanie for tonight
  • Refillable bottle - start the 3 L/day habit
  • Sunglasses: high-altitude light is fierce

Eat here

  • Quinoa soup - the altitude-day classic
  • Mate de coca on arrival, and again at dinner
